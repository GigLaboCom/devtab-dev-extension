Release notes, version 2.4

Highlights
- Exported reports keep their column widths and their sort order.
- Faster start-up on large projects.
- The search panel remembers the last query.

Fixes
- A crash when a folder name ended with a space.
- Tooltips no longer stay open after the window loses focus.
- The progress bar showed 99 % for finished tasks.
- Undo after a paste could restore the wrong selection.

Known issues
- Printing a report with more than 5 000 rows is slow.

Removed
- The old export format (.rpt). Open such files in 2.3 and save them again.

Upgrading
Install the new version over the old one. Settings are kept.
Back up your templates first if you edited them by hand.
Version 2.4 reads projects saved by any 2.x version.

Thanks to everyone who reported problems on the forum.
